    § 02 · How to borrow

    From shelf to your bag, and back

    1. Find a free book

      Look for the green Available or blue Returned bookmark on a book below.

    2. Ask or reserve

      Message the lender from the book. While we're at home, tap Reserve to be first in line when campus resumes.

    3. Tap Borrow at pick-up

      Enter your borrower code and the due date you agreed. Check the condition together, then set a return reminder.

    4. Tap Return

      Hand the book back by the due date, then tap Return with your code.

    § 03 · Status

    Every book wears a bookmark

    Every book shows one of these, updated live, so you can read the shelf at a glance.

    • Available

      With the lender, free to borrow.

    • Borrowed

      Someone has it. Their code and due date are on the book.

    • Reserved

      Promised to the next person in line.

    • Returned

      Back with the lender, free again.

    • Overdue

      Past its due date. Two days before, it shows Due soon in yellow.

    § 04 · Returns

    If a book doesn't come back

    Every loan has a due date, an automatic late flag, and a clear chain of people who follow up. Lenders lend with confidence; borrowers know what's expected.

    SHELF RULES

    Borrowing card

    1. Due date is set at pick-up. Lender and borrower agree it, and the borrower enters it when tapping Borrow.
    2. Late books are flagged. The shelf marks a book Overdue the day after it's due, by borrower code only.
    3. Reminders are built in. Borrowers set a calendar reminder at pick-up. Two days before the due date, the book shows Due soon and the lender sends a one-tap reminder from its card.
    4. Then follow-up. Still out 3 days after the due date, the shelf keeper follows up. After a week, the class rep steps in.
    5. One overdue, no new loans. A borrower with an overdue book can't borrow another until it's back.
    6. Lender needs it back. The borrower returns it within 2 days of being asked.
    7. Borrowing is free. Nobody pays to borrow. Lost or damaged: the borrower replaces it or settles a fair amount with the lender. Agree the book's condition at pick-up, especially for older books.
    § 05 · Who does what

    Responsibility is shared

    Borrowers

    Tap Borrow and Return themselves, and bring books back on time in the same condition.

    Lenders

    Set their book's terms, check its condition at hand-over, and send the first reminder if it's late.

    Shelf keeper

    Class rep

    Approved the shelf and handles anything unresolved, including books that stay overdue.

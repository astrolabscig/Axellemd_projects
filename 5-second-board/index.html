The 5-Second Board

A shared noticeboard for the whole department, online. Anyone can pin a notice for everyone to see. Each notice fits in four lines and takes itself down on its end date, so old papers never bury new ones.

Prototype by Joseph Boafo Afful for The Hidden Why (E360, Be Curious). Sample notices are fictional. Notices you post are saved only on this device.

Post a notice

Four lines. If it can't be read in 5 seconds, it's too long.
